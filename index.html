A six-point quality check for every guide

Whether you begin with Yono Games, Yono Rummy, or a Spin-focused page, the same checks make the reading process more consistent.

Clear purpose: Can you describe what the page helps you learn in one sentence?
Useful structure: Do headings make the important sections easy to locate?
Device relevance: Are platform or compatibility notes specific enough to check?
Permission awareness: Does the guide encourage careful review of requested access?
Information freshness: Are there signals that instructions and interface references are current?
Independent verification: Can important statements be confirmed through a relevant source?
